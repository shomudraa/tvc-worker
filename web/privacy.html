How this works

You upload one photo of your own face. We check it contains exactly one real face, remove its hidden metadata, and send only the seconds of the film where the actor appears to an AI face swap service, together with your photo.

Your photo is deleted from our servers the moment the AI service returns the result. The finished video is stored for 24 hours so you can download or share it, then it is deleted automatically.

We keep a record that you agreed to this (time, a hashed version of your connection address, and the version of this notice) for 24 hours, and nothing else. We do not use your face to train anything.
